## Survival System — Alpha 0.1 Rules

Alpha survival is designed to be beginner-friendly. It introduces survival concepts without punishing new players.

### Active Survival Mechanics (Alpha 0.1)

- Hunger (slow depletion)
- Thirst (slow depletion)
- Basic food crafting/finding
- Basic water sources
- Shelter bonus (inn/campfire gives comfort buff)
- Light injury concepts (healable at inn or with herbs)
- Low weather pressure

### NOT Active in Alpha 0.1

- Harsh environmental death
- Temperature systems
- Disease mechanics
- Full weather damage
- Survival permadeath

---

## Survival Design Principles

- Survival pressure adds challenge, not frustration
- Beginners should be able to learn without dying constantly
- Food and water should be readily available near starter towns
- Inn / campfire should always be a safe fallback
- Survival mechanics should feel atmospheric, not punishing

---

## Extraction System — Harvest Hollow Alpha 0.1 Rules

### Active Extraction Rules

- PvE only — no player vs player extraction
- No permadeath
- No full inventory wipe on failure
- Unstable loot (gathered inside dungeon before extraction) may be lost on failed extraction
- Secured loot (carried in from outside) remains safe
- Quest-critical items are recoverable

### NOT Active in Alpha 0.1

- Full inventory wipe
- Full bank wipe
- Permanent death
- PvP extraction

---

## Extraction Do-Not Rules

Do NOT mutate final inventory, bank, or save data unless:
- A safe existing inventory system exists
- Owner explicitly approves the mutation
- It is specifically included in an approved patch

---

## Survival Skills (All Beginner-Accessible)

All core survival skills must be accessible at Alpha 0.1 without race locks or faction locks:
- cooking
- herbalism
- survival
- farming
- fishing

---

## Hunger/Thirst Meter

Meters deplete slowly.
At zero: mild stat debuffs only (no insta-death in Alpha 0.1).
Recovery: any food item, water source, or inn service.
