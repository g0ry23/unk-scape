## Oathstead Village — Character

- Blood Oath influenced
- Rough, warm, survival-driven
- Practical, firelit, field labor
- Oath-family bonds
- Local grit
- Accessible NPC services

---

## Highmere Keep — Character

- Highborn influenced
- Ordered, patrolled, taxed
- Stone-built, legalistic
- Safer roads
- Structured services
- Polished hierarchy

---

## Harvest Hollow — Character

- Starter PvE extraction dungeon
- Old field cellar, collapsed root tunnels
- Stolen harvest stores
- Candlelit warnings
- Unstable hollow crates
- Beginner danger level

---

## Hearthvale Beginner Facilities

All of these should be accessible in Alpha 0.1:

- Combat yard
- Shallow mine
- Forge / anvil
- Woodlot
- Riverbank (fishing)
- Cooking fire
- Inn kitchen
- Herb fields
- Apothecary
- Crafting shed
- Starter farming plots
- Hunting trails
- Claim tutorial plot
- Bank
- General vendor
- Dungeon broker
- Harvest Hollow entry
